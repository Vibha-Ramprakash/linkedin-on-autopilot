How it works

Claude reads your old posts, learns how you actually write, and saves it as one file called voice.md. A free pack of 12 LinkedIn skills uses that file to plan your week, write the drafts and strip out anything that sounds like AI. Typefully is the bridge to LinkedIn. Nothing goes out until you say yes.

📄 voice.md
Your rulebook. How you open, your rhythm, your words, what's banned.
🧩 12 LinkedIn skills
Post Writer, Humanizer, Post Audit, Content Planner, Hook Extractor and 7 more. Free, open source.
📤 Typefully
Claude puts drafts straight into your Typefully. You read, tweak, then publish or schedule.
🗂 A dashboard
One page with your week, your voice map and every draft. See mine.

Method 1 · Apify

  1. Make a free account at apify.com.
  2. In Claude: Settings → Connectors, add the Apify connector and sign in.
  3. Paste this, with your profile link:
Prompt · ApifyConnect to my Apify account and run the harvestapi/linkedin-profile-posts actor on linkedin.com/in/YOUR-PROFILE Pull my last 20 posts. Original posts only, no reposts. For each one, give me the full text, the date and the reactions. Then use them to build my voice map.
If Apify says "monthly usage hard limit exceeded", your free credits are used up for the month. Switch to Method 2. That's exactly what happened to me while testing this.

Method 2 · Claude in Chrome

  1. Install the Claude in Chrome extension and sign in.
  2. Be logged into LinkedIn in that Chrome.
  3. Paste this:
Prompt · Claude in ChromeOpen my LinkedIn in Chrome and go to linkedin.com/in/YOUR-PROFILE/recent-activity/all Scroll until you've seen my last 20 original posts. Skip reposts. Copy the full text, date and reactions of each one, then use them to build my voice map. Only read. Don't like, comment or post anything.

Method 3 · Agent Reach

Runs in Claude Code. It needs a one-time LinkedIn login, and Claude will walk you through it.

Prompt · Agent ReachInstall Agent Reach from github.com/Panniantong/Agent-Reach and set up its LinkedIn channel. Walk me through any login step. Then use it to pull my last 20 LinkedIn posts from linkedin.com/in/YOUR-PROFILE, with the full text, date and reactions. If it can't fetch posts directly, use its web reader on my activity page. Then build my voice map.
STEP 2

Map your voice, save voice.md

Once Claude has your posts, run this in the same chat. You get a visual voice map plus a voice.md file that every skill reads from now on.

Prompt · Voice mapRead my last 20 LinkedIn posts and build me a voice map as a one-page artifact. Show me: 1. A radar chart of me vs default AI on honest, direct, warm, story-led, real numbers and casual. Score each 0 to 100 with one line of proof from my posts. 2. A word cloud of the words I actually use, sized by how often. 3. The words I never use, crossed out. 4. My rhythm: my sentence lengths vs a typical AI post. 5. My signature moves, with a count out of 20. Then save it all as voice.md, so everything you write for me sounds like me.

What mine found (19 real posts)

  • My 2024 posts opened like press releases ("Kickstart Your 2025 B2B SaaS Marketing Strategy!"). My 2026 ones open with a scene ("It's 11:43pm. On a Friday.").
  • 12 of 19 posts had a real number. 3.6 emojis per post. My most-used word is "real".
  • It caught 14 dashes. Readers clock dashes as AI now, so they're banned.
Check the map against reality. My first demo map said I end 14 of 20 posts on a question. The real number was 1. Always read the counts before you trust them.

Add your own rules to voice.md

Prompt · RulesAdd these rules to my voice.md: - Open with a scene or a moment, never an announcement - One real number per post, and it must be true - CAPS for one word, max - No dashes. Use commas and full stops - 3 emojis max - Never invent a story, a stat or a client. If you need one, ask me - You draft. I approve. Nothing posts without my yes

STEP 4

Connect Typefully

  1. Sign up at typefully.com and connect your LinkedIn there first.
  2. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  3. Paste https://mcp.typefully.com/mcp, click Add and sign in to Typefully.
  4. Test it:
Prompt · Test the connectionCheck my Typefully connection. Which accounts are connected, and how many publishes do I have left this month?
Mine said: LinkedIn connected, 10 publishes left this month. Typefully saves everything as a draft unless you clearly tell it to publish. Publishing now always asks you to confirm first.
STEP 5

Plan and draft your week

Bring your own ideas. Mine came from a WhatsApp note to myself: "Who's actually going to pay for AI?", "Is AI cheaper than DIY?", "No one wants anything new anymore". Your opinions are the part AI can't make up.

Prompt · Draft the weekUse my voice.md and the LinkedIn skills. Here are my 5 ideas for this week: 1. [idea] 2. [idea] 3. [idea] 4. [idea] 5. [idea] Plan Monday to Friday, one post a day at 9:00. Write each draft in my voice, run the humanizer, then the post audit. Ground any facts in real sources and list the sources for each post. If a post needs a personal story or number you don't have, ask me instead of inventing one. Save all 5 to my Typefully as drafts. Don't schedule or publish anything.
STEP 6

Make a carousel

LinkedIn carousels are PDFs. Claude can design the slides as a web page, turn them into images, stitch them into a PDF and attach it to your Typefully draft.

Prompt · CarouselMake a 4-slide LinkedIn carousel for my post "[post title]". Slide 1: a hook that stops the scroll. Slides 2 to 3: one piece of evidence each, with a big number, a simple visual and a small source line. Slide 4: the takeaway and a question. Style: [describe your brand, or attach 2 slides you've made before]. Size 1080x1350. Render each slide as an image, combine them into one PDF, upload it to Typefully and attach it to that draft. Then show me the slides.
Put the sources in the first comment, not the post. It keeps the post clean and makes you look like you did your homework (because you did).
STEP 7

Review, then publish

Open each draft in Typefully. Read it out loud. If it doesn't sound like you, fix it or ask Claude to redo it. Then:

Prompt · PublishShow me exactly what will go out for "[post title]": the text, the attachment and the first comment. Then wait for me to say "yes, publish".
Prompt · Or scheduleSchedule my approved drafts in Typefully for 9:00 on their days. Leave anything I haven't approved as a draft.
STEP 8

Your dashboard

Open my dashboard ↗ It plays my week on its own: drafting, humanizing, scheduling. It has 5 tabs: Live draft, My voice, 12 skills, Humanizer and This week. The data is mine, as a demo.

To build yours, paste this into Claude after steps 1 to 5:

Prompt · Build my dashboardBuild me a one-page LinkedIn dashboard as an artifact, using my real data from this chat. Tabs: 1. My voice: my voice map from voice.md, plus a searchable table of the posts it learned from, with dates and impressions. 2. 12 skills: which LinkedIn skills I have installed and what each one did this week. 3. This week: a Monday to Friday calendar of my Typefully drafts. Click a day to read the full post, edit it, and see its status (draft, waiting for me, scheduled, published). 4. Humanizer: before and after for one draft, with the AI tells crossed out. Keep it clean and on brand: [your colours]. Don't add any button that publishes without asking me first.
Tip for filming: make the dashboard full screen, then record. My demo version has a ↺ replay button and a speed setting for exactly this.

The rules I learned building this

  • It drafts, I approve. Nothing auto-posts. Ever.
  • Don't post on LinkedIn that AI writes your LinkedIn. Post your opinions. The setup is the how, not the content.
  • Never let it invent a story or a stat. If a draft says "I counted 9 apps today", make sure you did.
  • Ground hot takes in real research and list the sources in the first comment.
  • Check the counts. The voice map can be confidently wrong. Read it against your real posts.
  • Re-check the skill pack before you share it. It went from 11 to 12 skills in a few weeks.
  • Bring the ideas yourself. A note to yourself on WhatsApp is enough. AI is great at drafting and terrible at having your opinions.
